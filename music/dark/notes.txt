DARK: what went into the track and its sound effects
======================================================
Source: sfx.js as copied on 2026-11-14 (songs.js, instruments.js, sound-effects.js in this folder) and the
project notes in CLAUDE.md. What all five tracks share is in ../README.txt.

IDENTITY
  Hard warehouse techno, "intense". It was reimagined once, into this. The colour theme it pairs with is Red.
  Key: E Phrygian. Songs are written in A Phrygian and moved down a fourth (`shift` -5). The flat second
  (F over E) is the track's sound: it is allowed to rub where the other tracks avoid semitones.

THE INSTRUMENTS (instruments.js)
  Kick      Punchy: 170 to 42 Hz, the loudest kick of the five.
  Rumble    A low saw on the chord's root on the three 16ths after each kick, fading (0.12, 0.09, 0.06).
            Its filter was raised (240 Hz and up) so it can be heard on small speakers.
  Acid      A saw through a very resonant filter (Q 12) that snaps shut on every note: roots, the octave,
            the flat second, a third and a fifth above the chord's root, with accents on the turns
            (DARK_ACID, DARK_ACCENT). The second half of every four-bar phrase answers with its own line
            (DARK_ACID_B). Its turns are snapped into the scale. It plays at rest too, quieter, and opens
            with speed. Each song sets how much of it there is (`acid`).
            The rumble and the acid line are this track's bass: there is no separate bass line.
  Hats      Open hats on the off-beats, then a ride of 16ths.
  Snare     A clap layered with a metal hat on 2 and 4.
  Pad       Detuned saws (9 cents either side) an octave down, held a whole bar with a slow attack.
  Stabs     Short resonant saw stabs on a song's `comp` steps; when the top layer is in, a three-note
            detuned hoover stab of the chord every other bar.
  Lead      A hoover: two saws 12 cents either side.
  Fill      A snare roll with metal and a noise riser into the next phrase.
  Mood      The master filter runs 1,400 to 9,400 Hz. A light constant drive (0.04): a heavier one played
            at the same level however the parts were set, which kept the drums loud.

THE SONGS (songs.js; chords as written, in A: they sound a fourth lower)
  Foundry (lab)       Am Am Bb Am Dm Dm Bb C. A low pad, the acid line held back (0.45).
  Rust (lab)          Am Gm F Gm Am Bb F Bb. A slow descent, i-vii-VI-vii; acid at 0.35.
  Warehouse (server)  Am Am Bb Bb Am Am F Gm. The acid line at full tilt, a hoover riff on a dotted rhythm.
  Pressure (server)   Am Bb Am Gm Am Bb Dm Bb. A low hoover (an octave down) stabbing, stabs on the
                      off-beats.
  Black Box (snare)   Am Am Bb Bb Am Am Gm Gm. "The warehouse held back": the acid line low and closed
                      (0.3), the rumble at 0.6, the kick a pulse on 1 and 3, the flat second waiting.
  Tripwire (snare)    Am Bb Am Bb Dm C Dm Bb. A against B flat, a hoover creeping up the D minor, never
                      home. Acid 0.25, rumble 0.5.
  All triads, no sevenths: the weight is in the timbre, not the chord. The chords move by step around the
  flat second (Am to Bb) instead of by fifths. Melodies were checked against their chords, the flat
  seconds and flat ninths excepted on purpose.
  In the stealth songs the acid line and the rumble open up as you're spotted, on top of the shared
  hushed kick, clock tick and closed filter (`cut` 0.7).

LEVELS (measured K-weighted; see ../README.txt)
  Overall trim 0.94. Beat 0.31, tones 3.49: the widest correction of the five. Measured, the drums sat
  far over the tones and Dark's tones were inaudible; now the drums are 2 dB under the tones.
  Song `lvl` 0.77 to 1.05. The stealth songs lift their tones the most while hushed (`rest` 2.01, 2.20).
  Simple version: gain 1, beat 1.032, tones 3.515, a saw bass.

SOUND EFFECTS (sound-effects.js)
  Key for effects: E Phrygian's five notes E F G B C. Each effect is written in A minor pentatonic and each
  degree moves to the same degree here, so a figure keeps its shape and gains the flat second.
  Profile (for any effect Dark doesn't write itself): triangles and squares become saws, sines become
  triangles, an octave down, filters 30% more closed, through a drive; noise louder and darker.
  Its own instruments: `metal` (a short ring of narrow-band noise) and `saw` (a driven, resonant saw).
  The idea: industrial. Distorted saws, metal hits, sub drops, flat seconds.
  Written again for Dark (18 general effects and all 29 Packet Snare ones)
    Clicks    compute a short saw with a tick, data a metal tick on a thin square, research a detuned,
              driven triangle an octave down.
    Buys      three-note figures built on the flat second; by panel: driven saws (hardware, legacy), thin
              squares with a metal tick (software, consciousness, Packet Snare), detuned triangles (research,
              the skill tree). A big buy adds a sub drop and falling noise.
    Error     E and F together: a semitone cluster.
    Sprint    a resonant noise riser and a driven sub drop.
    Prompts   clean: two squares and a metal tick. Super: a saw run that falls from the flat second.
    Summit    a held saw chord with the flat second in it, a sub drop, a noise wash, two metal hits. It is a
              weight landing, where Classic's is a rhythm.
    Also      claim, ui, achievement, land, launch, run end, record, near miss, breakthrough, phase, ship.
  Played through the profile instead (Classic's notes, Dark's colour): system prompt, smash, wind-up,
  crunch, flow, freeze, release, viral.
  Packet Snare: "held back": driven saws muted low, metal ticks, sub thumps, the flat second for alarms.
  Sprint meter: the shared four parts through the profile's saws. Its levels are capped: Dark's dense mix
  would have needed more than the ceiling allows, so the meter stands about 4 dB over the music, not 7.
  Simple sounds: a filtered saw.
  Levels: SFX_TRIM 1.29, the simple set 4.65 (the largest simple trim).

CALLS AND CONSTRAINTS ON RECORD
  - Dark's tones were inaudible at the owner's Beat setting; the part balance above is the fix.
  - The acid line plays at rest (quieter) so the track is recognisable before the layers build.
  - The rumble is filtered high enough for small speakers.
  - The drive stays light, and is routed past the sounds' volume.
