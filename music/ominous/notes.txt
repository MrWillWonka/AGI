OMINOUS: what went into the track and its sound effects
=========================================================
Source: sfx.js as copied on 2026-11-14 (songs.js, instruments.js, sound-effects.js in this folder) and the
project notes in CLAUDE.md. What all five tracks share is in ../README.txt.

IDENTITY
  Slow dread. The colour theme it pairs with is Void.
  Key: C harmonic minor, leaning on the flat second (D flat). Songs are written in A harmonic minor an
  octave down and moved up a minor third (`shift` +3).
  The one track with a different feel on the shared grid: half-time.

THE INSTRUMENTS (instruments.js)
  Kick      Deep and long (110 to 32 Hz over 0.6 s), on beats 1 and 3 only.
  Drone     A low saw on the chord's root, held the whole bar: the bass doesn't move inside a bar.
  Bell      A sine with a quiet partial a twelfth up, tolling a chord tone every other bar. The tone is
            voice-led: it takes the chord tone nearest the last one it rang.
  Clock     Quiet hat ticks on the off-beats; even quieter 16ths later.
  Clap      Only on beat 4, at half Classic's level.
  Strings   The chord in triangles an octave down, held for the bar.
  Lead      A triangle tuned 10 cents flat that swells in (up to 80 ms of attack), with a faint sine bell an
            octave above it. The harmony line is tuned 10 cents sharp, so the two beat against each other.
  Arpeggio  One triangle note of the chord on each beat.
  Fill      Only a reversed swell (a noise riser) into the next phrase. No drum fill.
  Mood      The master filter runs 500 to 4,000 Hz, the darkest of the five. No drive at all: a saturating
            drive kept the drums loud however the parts were set.

THE SONGS (songs.js; chords as written, in A: they sound a minor third higher)
  Vigil (lab)         Am Am Bb Bb Dm Dm E7 E7. Half notes, the flat second in the line, E7's flat ninth.
  Static (lab)        Am Dm Am Bb Am Dm F E. "A motif that keeps falling back to the flat second."
  Descent (server)    Am Bb Am Bb Fmaj7 Dm Bb E7. A against B flat, then down through F to E.
  Undertow (server)   Am Am Bb C Am Am Bb E7. A dotted figure climbing the chord and back: the only
                      rhythmic melody in the track.
  Crawlspace (snare)  Am Bb Am Bb Dm Bb E E. A heartbeat for a kick (two hits, the second softer), the bell
                      far off.
  Lights Out (snare)  Am F Dm E Am F Bb E7. The line stepping down and stopping to listen.
  Triads plus the dominant seventh. The harmony is the Neapolitan (B flat against A minor) and the
  dominant (E), so every phrase ends pulling home and never rests. Melodies are the slowest in the game:
  half notes and whole notes. Checked against their chords, with the flat seconds and flat ninths left in
  on purpose.
  Stealth songs: the hushed kick is louder here than elsewhere (60%), with the shared tick and closed
  filter (`cut` 0.8).

LEVELS (measured K-weighted; see ../README.txt)
  Overall trim 1.42. Beat 0.598, tones 2.145: drums 2 dB under the tones.
  Song `lvl` 0.88 to 0.95, the tightest spread; stealth `rest` only 1.14 and 1.22, because there are few
  drums to miss.
  Simple version: gain 1, beat 1.378, tones 1.951, a low sine bass, the kick on 1 and 3.

SOUND EFFECTS (sound-effects.js)
  Key for effects: C, D flat, E flat, F sharp, G. The flat second and the tritone are built into the
  scale, so every figure written for Classic comes out unsettled here without being rewritten.
  Profile (for any effect Ominous doesn't write itself): squares become triangles, an octave down, filters
  55% more closed, 70% longer, a slow attack (20 ms), tuned 14 cents flat, and every note answered by an
  echo a dotted 8th later at 30%; noise longer and much darker.
  Its own instruments: `toll` (a long sine with an inharmonic partial at 2.76 times the note: a real bell's
  clang), `knock` (a muffled triangle with a noise thud), `swellUp` (noise that swells and cuts off: a
  reversed sound).
  The idea: tolling bells, knocks, reversed swells and drones.
  Written again for Ominous (18 general effects and all 29 Packet Snare ones)
    Clicks    compute a knock, data a ghostly ping with its echo (quieter on phones), research a short toll
              an octave down.
    Buys      two-note figures, slow: tolls (hardware, legacy), high detuned sines with echoes (software,
              consciousness, Packet Snare), detuned triangles swelling in (research, the skill tree). A big
              buy is a reversed swell into a sub drop.
    Error     a semitone cluster in saws.
    Sprint    a reversed swell, and the sub drop lands after it, later the bigger the charge.
    Prompts   clean: one long sine with its echo. Super: two tolls.
    Summit    a low toll, a slow minor cluster swelling in, a high toll answering, a long sub.
    Ship      three tolls, each an octave above the last, over a long sub.
    Also      claim (tolls falling), ui, achievement, land (long and low), launch, run end, record, near
              miss, breakthrough, phase.
  Played through the profile instead: system prompt, smash, wind-up, crunch, flow, freeze, release, viral.
  Packet Snare: "creeping": muffled detuned triangles, knocks, a bell's warning down to the flat second, a
  deep heartbeat with a toll, reversed swells.
  Sprint meter: the shared four parts through the profile.
  Simple sounds: a low sine with a long tail, an octave down.
  Levels: SFX_TRIM 0.81 (the only track whose effects had to come down: long tails add up), simple 1.37.

CALLS AND CONSTRAINTS ON RECORD
  - Errors still sit above the basses' register.
  - Long tails overlap, so Ominous is the track most likely to stack sounds into the master compressor.
