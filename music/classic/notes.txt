CLASSIC: what went into the track and its sound effects
=========================================================
Source: sfx.js as copied on 2026-11-14 (songs.js, instruments.js, sound-effects.js in this folder) and the
project notes in CLAUDE.md. Classic is the original: every other track is measured against it and falls
back to its sound effects. What all five tracks share is in ../README.txt.

IDENTITY
  Dub techno. The colour theme it pairs with is Night survey.
  Key: A minor (natural minor), as written. Every song and effect in the game is written in A and moved
  into the other tracks' keys, so Classic is the only track that plays the notes on the page.

THE INSTRUMENTS (instruments.js)
  Kick      A sine drop 150 to 45 Hz on every beat (four on the floor).
  Bass      A saw on each song's own bass line; its filter opens with the run's speed (220 to 1,820 Hz).
  Hats      Off-beat hats, then 16th hats, each on its own layer.
  Clap      On beats 2 and 4.
  Chords    Dub chords: a filtered saw stab on the song's `comp` steps, answered a dotted 8th later by a
            quieter, darker copy (a delay throw written as a second note, not a delay effect).
  Lead      A filtered square, with the same dotted-8th throw at about a third of the level.
  Arpeggio  The chord in squares an octave up, on every 16th, starting a step later each bar so the figure
            turns over a four-bar phrase.
  Fill      Every eighth bar ends in a clap roll that gets louder, and a square note two octaves over the
            next chord's root on the last 16th.
  Mood      The master filter runs 900 Hz at rest to 9,900 Hz at full speed (a squared curve, so most of the
            opening comes late). No drive.

THE SONGS (songs.js)
  Lab songs are steadier and more melodic; server-room songs drive harder; Packet Snare's are stealth.
  Night Survey (lab)     Am9 Am9 Fmaj7 Fmaj7 Cmaj7 Cmaj7 Dm7 Cmaj7. Two bars a chord, chords on the off-beats
                         of 1 and 3, a long-breathed line. Never touches the dominant: it floats.
  Uptime (lab)           Am7 Fmaj9 C6 Gsus2 Am7 Fmaj9 Dm7 E7. i-VI-III-VII, "wide and hopeful", chords late
                         in the bar (the off-beats of 2 and 4). The E7 at the turn is the only strong pull
                         home in the lab, and the melody takes the G sharp with it.
  Overclock (server)     Am7 Am7 Dm7 Dm7 Fmaj7 G Esus4 E7. A rolling 16th bass, a short riff with rests,
                         three chord stabs a bar, harmonic minor's E7 at the turn.
  Packet Storm (server)  Am G Fmaj7 E7 Am G Dm9 E7. The Andalusian cadence under a busy hook, the bass
                         bouncing octaves on the 8ths. The only melody with 16th notes (bar 5 of B).
  Packet Sniffer (snare) Am9 Am9 Fmaj7 Fmaj7 Dm9 Dm9 Esus4 Esus4. Dub techno gone quiet: one muted stab a
                         bar, the phrase left hanging on a sus chord that never resolves.
  Dead Drop (snare)      Am7 Am7 Em7 Em7 Fmaj7 Fmaj7 Gsus4 Gsus4. A bass that steps and stops.
  Chords are sevenths and ninths rather than triads, voiced close to the last chord (smooth voice leading);
  five-note chords leave the root to the bass. Every melody note on a beat or held was checked against its
  chord: no semitone rubs.
  Stealth songs carry their own hushed kick pattern (`kick`, at 45% level), a quiet clock tick (`tick`) and a
  master filter that starts 25% closed (`cut` 0.75); as you're spotted the track's own kick fades in.

LEVELS (measured K-weighted in headless Chromium; see ../README.txt)
  Classic is the reference: no overall trim (gain 1). Beat 0.659, tones 1.97, which puts the drums 2 dB
  under the tones (the owner kept Beat at 50% or lower; the drums had sat 4 to 12.5 dB over the tones).
  Each song's `lvl` (0.82 to 1.01) evens the songs; the stealth songs need more (1.37 and 1.62) and a
  further lift while their drums are hushed (`rest` 1.36 to 1.40).
  Simple version: gain 1, beat 0.987, tones 2.455, a triangle bass.

SOUND EFFECTS (sound-effects.js)
  Classic's set is the design every other track starts from: SOUNDS holds all 27 general effects and the
  29 Packet Snare ones. SFX_TRIM is 1 (the reference); the simple set is trimmed 2.33.
  House voices
    blip   a square through a 2.6 kHz lowpass, 90 ms: the house voice
    stab   detuned saws through a resonant lowpass: chords
    sub    a sine dropping or holding, mostly A1 (55 Hz) down to A0 (27.5 Hz)
    echo   the same figure again a dotted 8th later at 40%: the delay throw on prompts
    bell   a sine with a quiet partial a twelfth up; pluck: a short filtered saw
  The clicks (the most-heard sounds in the game)
    Compute   a round triangle in the middle register with a bright tick two octaves up and a noise tick.
              The owner's call: an octave lower it sat under the basses, and in the chords' register it
              read as part of the track, so the tick puts its energy where the music is thin.
    Data      the square blip, bright and short; on touch screens 45% quieter with a darker filter, because
              phone speakers push the upper mids.
    Research  a sine with its octave, high and bell-like.
    Each job plays an eight-note figure across the bar (CLICK_NOTES); where the click falls in the bar
    picks its note, so tapping or holding plays a tune in time. The three are levelled alike, data about
    5 dB under compute, and each clears the music by 6 dB or more in its own third-octave bands.
  Buying: one sound per panel, three or four variations that never repeat back to back
    hardware square blips (with a sub on a big buy), software quick high triangles like a terminal,
    research bells, consciousness a soft saw pad, the skill tree saw plucks, legacy a wide chord with a
    sub, Packet Snare's tree glassy triangle runs.
  Training
    sprint: a noise riser as strong as the charge, and a sub drop to A.
    clean / super / system prompt: two, four, then nine rising blips, each with the delay throw.
    breakthrough: a sub drop, a noise burst, an A minor stab on the next 16th. smash: the same, bigger.
    summit (a skill learned): stabs on the 16ths, Am, Am, G, then Am held under a high E with a sub.
    near miss: one note sagging a whole tone. flow: a slow A minor arpeggio in sines.
  Errors sit an octave above the basses so they can be heard.
  Packet Snare (TRACK_SOUNDS.classic): written apart from the training sounds on purpose, "in the stealth
  spirit": muted saw plucks low down for a catch (two to six notes by the multiplier), ticks, filtered
  noise, a heartbeat sub for the boss, the delay throw on a few. Levelled against Classic's own 17 effects
  by SNARE_TRIM (frequent ones 3.5 dB under, alarms 3.5 over, the boss 5.5 over).
  The sprint meter: the four shared parts (arpeggio, octave hops, acid line, gated chord) in triangles and
  saws, levelled per part by CHARGE_TRIM to stand about 7 dB over the music.
  Simple sounds: a filtered square (the blip, plain).

CALLS AND CONSTRAINTS ON RECORD
  - Compute took data's old sound (the owner's call).
  - The sprint meter never plays a rising tone (the owner found the old glide grating): it plays notes of
    the chord the music is on.
  - Every sound lands on the grid: the next 16th, big stingers and held clicks the next 8th.
  - The beat plays only while training (the owner's call); elsewhere the music is its tones alone.
  - Nothing plays the same way twice running (the generate clicks excepted).
