SOFT: what went into the track and its sound effects
======================================================
Source: sfx.js as copied on 2026-11-14 (songs.js, instruments.js, sound-effects.js in this folder) and the
project notes in CLAUDE.md. What all five tracks share is in ../README.txt.

IDENTITY
  "Subtle", "deep and gentle". The colour theme it pairs with is Soft pink.
  Key: F major / D minor. Songs are written around C major or A minor and moved up a fourth (`shift` +5).
  The lab songs are major (the only major-key lab besides Retro's); the server songs are minor and Dorian.

THE INSTRUMENTS (instruments.js)
  Kick      Soft and short: 110 to 48 Hz at about two thirds of Classic's level.
  Bass      A round triangle on each song's own line, three 16ths long, with a low filter.
  Pad       The whole chord in triangles, held for the bar.
  Rhodes    Soft sines an octave up on the song's `comp` steps.
  Shakers   Very quiet, on the odd 16ths.
  Rim       On beat 4 only (in place of a clap).
  Lead      A kalimba: a sine with a bright 70 ms tick an octave up. Notes are capped at 0.9 s.
  Arpeggio  Sines on the 8ths, rising for two bars and falling for two.
  In plain terms, the names promise more than the synthesis delivers: the "Rhodes" is one sine oscillator
  per note with a volume envelope, the "kalimba" one sine plus a brief octave tone, the "shaker" and "rim"
  the same noise hat at two lengths. None has the attack, the overtones or the decay of the real thing.  Fill      A rim pattern and the next chord's top note, leading in.
  Mood      The master filter runs 700 to 5,200 Hz: the darkest ceiling after Ominous. No drive.

THE SONGS (songs.js; chords as written: they sound a fourth higher)
  Morning Lab (lab)       Cmaj7 Am7 Fmaj7 G6 Cmaj7 Em7 Fmaj7 Gsus4. Major sevenths, the Rhodes on the
                          off-beats of 2 and 4, the kalimba line an octave down.
  Window Seat (lab)       Fmaj7 G Em7 Am7 Dm7 G Cmaj7 Cmaj7. IV-V-iii-vi, then ii-V-I: "unhurried", and the
                          only song in the game that ends its phrase resolved on the tonic for two bars.
  Gentle Climb (server)   Am9 Fmaj7 Cadd9 G6 Dm9 Fmaj7 Cadd9 Esus4. Minor ninths, a syncopated bass.
  Glide Path (server)     Am7 D9 Am7 D9 Fmaj7 G Am7 Esus4. Dorian (the bright sixth), "a funky Rhodes on
                          every off-beat": the busiest comp in the track.
  Tiptoe (snare)          Am9 Am9 Gsus2 Gsus2 Fmaj7 Fmaj7 Esus4 Esus4. A mallet whispering low, the bass on
                          one and three.
  Hushed Channel (snare)  Fmaj7 Fmaj7 Dm9 Dm9 Am7 Am7 Gsus4 Gsus4. Major sevenths drifting to a sus chord
                          that never lands, the kalimba kept low.
  The richest harmony of the five (ninths, sixths, add9, sus). Melodies move mostly in quarter notes with
  long held notes at phrase ends. Every melody note on a beat or held was checked against its chord: no
  semitone rubs.
  Stealth songs: the shared hushed kick (50%), clock tick and closed filter (`cut` 0.8).

LEVELS (measured K-weighted; see ../README.txt)
  Overall trim 1.7 (the largest: soft sines and triangles carry little energy). Beat 0.738, tones 1.487:
  drums 2 dB under the tones.
  Song `lvl` 0.77 to 0.91; stealth 1.14 and 1.27 with `rest` 1.32 and 1.26.
  Simple version: gain 1, beat 0.997, tones 2.032, a sine bass.

SOUND EFFECTS (sound-effects.js)
  Key for effects: F major pentatonic (F G A C D). The scale has no semitones in it, so effects don't rub
  against each other; they can still rub against a chord (an F over Am7's E, or over C6), since effects
  follow the scale and not the chord.
  Profile (for any effect Soft doesn't write itself): squares and saws become triangles, filters 45% more
  closed, a slower attack (12 ms), 25% longer, noise 30% quieter.
  Its own instruments: `marimba` (a sine with a short tick two octaves up), `softBell` (a long sine with a
  triangle an octave up), `padChord` (triangles swelling in over 150 ms).
  The idea: mallets and bells, a warm pad.
  Written again for Soft (18 general effects and all 29 Packet Snare ones)
    Clicks    compute a marimba, data a glassy kalimba (quieter on phones), research a bell.
    Buys      rising three-note figures; marimba (hardware, legacy), glassy high sines (software,
              consciousness, Packet Snare), soft bells (research, the skill tree). A big buy adds a pad chord.
    Error     a sine falling a third. No dissonance, even for a mistake.
    Sprint    soft lowpassed noise and a sine that rises an octave: where Classic and Dark drop, Soft lifts.
    Prompts   clean: two soft bells. Super: five overlapping sines, a strummed chord.
    Summit    a pad chord, four bells climbing, a low sine swelling under them.
    Breakthrough  a slow swell, then a pad chord and a bell: no sub drop and no impact.
    Also      claim (bells falling), ui, achievement, land, launch, run end (two marimba notes, up for good,
              down otherwise), record, near miss, phase, ship.
  Played through the profile instead: system prompt, smash, wind-up, crunch, flow, freeze, release, viral.
  Packet Snare: "secretive": muted triangles and glassy sines, soft bells, a sine heartbeat, a warm pad.
  Sprint meter: the shared four parts through the profile's triangles.
  Simple sounds: a sine that rings like a mallet (40% longer).
  Levels: SFX_TRIM 1.21, the simple set 1.56.

CALLS AND CONSTRAINTS ON RECORD
  - Data's click is quieter on touch screens in every track; here it is a glassy sine instead of a square.
  - Soft's Rhodes and shakers come in on the off-beat hats' layer, so the track has movement before any
    hats would have arrived.
