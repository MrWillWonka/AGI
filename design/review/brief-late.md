# Brief: the boss, the late game and performance (reviewer B)

Play what comes after the first twenty minutes, with the builds a person would have there: `K.scenario(page, k)` loads
sector k with the tree the pacing campaign had when it got there (not a maxed tree). Cover:
- the bosses on sectors 5, 8 and 11 (since 17.5 a boss every third sector from 5: it comes once Focus first falls to half,
  after a BOSS INCOMING warning, swallows every packet on the field and stops the Focus clock; its fight has a 30 s clock:
  beat it and the packets burst back out, run it out and it escapes and the run ends). Loops to stun, slashes, Riposte if
  owned; on 8 its mines (laid on a ring round it since 17.7: grab one with a bubble, flick it and it flies as a puck,
  bouncing off the edges, each bounce before a hit adding damage; Ricochet adds bounces), on 11 the nets as well. Several
  attempts each, noting why each failed;
- sectors 6–12 with their builds: Full Scans, the Rogue AI, the helpers (bots and towers), how the field reads, whether
  the player still makes decisions or watches;
- the tree in late game: what's worth buying, dead or confusing nodes, the endless Overclock;
- performance: the report's full-field frame rate, and your own on sector 12 with its scenario build.

Play on desktop 1280 × 800, with one boss fight and one late field on phone 390 × 727.

Score all seven categories as **Late** (what you played). Don't score Early.

Start from the kit's tours (`K.tour.boss(page, dir, 5)`, `(…, 8)` and `(…, 11)`, `K.tour.sector(page, k, dir)` for 6–12, `K.tour.tree(page, dir, 12)`); then play on by hand where they leave questions.
