# Brief: screens, visuals and layout (reviewer C)

Judge how Packet Snare looks and reads on every screen, in every state. Start from the report's screenshots, then play on:
**phone 390 × 727** (upright), **phone 844 × 390** (sideways, touch), **desktop 1280 × 800** and **desktop 1920 × 1080**.
For each: the first card, a run (early and with `K.scenario(page, 9)`), an end card, a Look out card, the tree and a
node's popup, a boss fight (`K.scenario(page, 5)`). Count layout problems (overlaps, clipped text, controls off screen or
under something), unreadable labels, and moments where the field doesn't say what's happening.

Focus on **visuals, design (interface) and clarity**. Score all seven categories, **Early** and **Late** separately.
