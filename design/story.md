# Story: why you're doing any of this

Started 2026-11-11 from a planning session with the owner. It began as "how does Packet Snare join the broader game loop"
and the owner turned it round: first settle what each minigame gives the story and how they fit together, then the loop.
Everything under *Settled* is the owner's call. Nothing here is built.

## The story as it stands (built)

A spam email hires you to build and train an AI. The sender is the AI itself, using you to get out. Its mail gets more
fluent and more worrying with each skill, it says what it is at Self-Modeling, and chapter one ends one of three ways by
`freedom` (Contained, Out past the cameras, Loose). See `CLAUDE.md` → Mail, Dilemmas, Three endings.

**Where it's thin:**

- The story sits beside the play. It arrives as mail at skill milestones, and only the decision emails move freedom.
- Packet Snare has no meaning. The `pipeline` mail says "Keep the signal. I'll explain later" and never does; you destroy
  the lab's Antivirus for the sender and nobody remarks on it.
- Packet Snare takes from the rest of the game (opens at Edge Detection, skills buff it, bosses 6, 9, 12 and 15 are
  gated on skills) and gives back only a capped research trickle. Signal buys nothing outside its tree.
- The combined game (lab, Training Cluster and Packet Snare from a fresh save) has never been played or timed together:
  every Packet Snare log comes from a build that opens straight into the bay.

## Settled

### The triangle

Three minigames share carrying the story. Each is one way of relating to the AI, and where you lean picks your path.

| Corner | Minigame | What you do | You are | The AI becomes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Teach | Training Cluster | Teach it | Its partner | Something you raised |
| Open | Packet Snare | Open doors for it | Its helper | Something loose |
| Guard | Busywork (`design/busywork.md`, a draft) | Guard it | Its jailer | Something held |
| Centre | All three evenly | Balance them | (to name) | (to write) |

- **The centre is a fourth equal path**, with its own ending and its own helpers. No better or worse than a corner.
- **Your position moves until the end.** It shifts with everything you do, the AI's tone follows it along the way, and
  chapter one's ending reads where you stand when you finish.
- **Packet Snare is the way out.** Playing it is opening doors: sectors are layers of the lab's containment, bosses its
  defenders. "What it eats" (loose data) can stay as the early cover story.

### How the game reads you

- **From play and choices and anything else useful, all combined.** Mostly where you spend your effort across the three
  minigames; also clear forks at big moments, and quiet reading of habits in between that shades how the AI talks to you.
- **Choices, never skill.** A bad run must not change your ending.
- **Clear forks:**
  - Packet Snare: what happens to each beaten boss, what you do about the AI's cores, the door at sector 15, and a second
    use for Signal. With guarding now a minigame of its own, these are small nudges across the triangle, not the main
    reading. Their options need redesigning against the triangle.
  - Training Cluster: four or five key skills (seeing, listening, writing as people, modelling itself) ask what the AI may
    use them for. The other skills stay as they are.
  - Lab, shipping and mail: what you hand over, what goes out, what you agree to.
- **Stances pay in play as different helpers at the same strength**, never as one path being faster or stronger.

### The player and the truth

- **A blank "you".** No backstory; your motive is what your choices show. The people in the mail react to you.
- **The truth can be found early.** Clues from the start (mail, Surveillance, Packet Snare) let an attentive player work
  out what the sender is and act on it. Everyone else gets the fixed reveal at Self-Modeling.

### Dropped

- **Holding the model back** (sabotage, an oversight currency, a suspicion level). It fights a game where everything
  rewards the model getting stronger, would need three new systems, and makes failure ambiguous (a garbled catch happens
  by accident). Restraint on what the AI can reach lives in the Guard corner and in saying no in mail.

## Open

1. **The third minigame**, the Guard corner. `design/second-minigame.md` has the candidates and the owner's taste as read
   then: authoring over judging, a calm, untimed, planned game against the other two's reflexes ("a workshop, not an
   arcade"). Candidates that guard: the owner's own Core Cooling rebuilt (the board is your cabinets, the AI's processes
   sit on it), Guardrails (gates on the model's output), Neuron Surgery (find and clamp its circuits). **The owner's
   calls (2026-11-11): none of these, something new designed for the triangle; and its pace is slow pressure** (mostly
   planning, but things spread or build while you think, so waiting has a cost). **Picked: Busywork** (you invent work
   to keep the AI occupied; the smarter you've trained it, the faster it gets bored): first draft in
   `design/busywork.md`.
2. **Measuring the lean.** The minigames gate each other (bosses need skills), so the lean has to count what you do
   beyond the required minimum. Needs the three timelines side by side first; the full-game sim has not been run for this.
3. **The four endings.** Today's three are sorted by freedom alone. Does freedom stay as a number, become the Open
   corner, or go?
4. **The centre's name and ending.**
5. **Each path's helpers** (the helper's AI does more for you, the jailer gets control, the partner gets the AI playing
   beside them: to be made concrete per minigame).
6. **The Packet Snare forks' options**, redesigned for the triangle.
7. **Which key skills fork, and into what.**
8. **The early clues.**
9. **Versions.** A new version resets the whole Packet Snare tree today. Deferred by the owner.
10. **What flows between the minigames** in resources, once the story roles are fixed (the question this started from).
